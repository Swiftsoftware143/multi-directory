# Visitor Guide

Welcome to ZaarHub — your local community marketplace.

## Finding Businesses
1. Go to **zaarhub.com** and click your city
2. Browse categories or use the search bar
3. Each listing shows name, category, rating, contact info

## Businesses vs Suppliers
Use the tab toggle at the top of each city page:
- **Businesses** — restaurants, plumbers, dentists, shops, services
- **Suppliers** — distributors, wholesalers, farms, trade associations

They're separated so you always find what you're looking for.

## Saving Businesses (Bookmarks)
Click the ♡ heart icon on any business listing to save it to your favorites.
- The icon turns ❤️ filled when saved
- View all your saved places at **zaarhub.com/saved-places**
- Remove a bookmark anytime by clicking the heart again
- Your saved places show with name, image, category, and a quick View button
- Bookmark count appears on each listing so you can see what's popular

## Claiming Deals
1. Click a deal on a business listing
2. Click the redemption button
3. Show the code to the business to redeem
4. Deal pages have countdown timers showing how long the offer lasts

## Creating an Account (Free)
Sign up to save favorites, claim deals, leave reviews, and get city newsletters.

## Leaving Reviews
Share your experience with local businesses by leaving a star rating and text review.
- Go to any business listing and find the Reviews section
- Click **Write a Review**, select 1–5 stars, add a title and your review text
- Submit — your review goes into a moderation queue before it appears publicly
- You can check your review's status on the same business listing page (it'll show as pending until approved)
- Once approved, your review appears on the listing with your name, rating, and date

## Messaging a Business
Contact businesses directly from their listing:
- Click the **Message** button on any business listing page
- Write your message and send — no account required for first contact
- If you have an account, you can see your message history with that business
- Messages go straight to the business's inbox in their dashboard

## Community Events
Your city page features an "Upcoming Events" section where you can browse what's happening locally.

### Browsing & Filtering
- View all events at your city's **/events** page — a full calendar view with upcoming events
- Filter by **date**, **category** (community, music, food, sports, business, workshop, other), or **free/paid**
- Each event card shows title, date/time, location, and attendee count

### RSVP & Attending
- Click **RSVP** on any event to mark yourself as going
- See who else is attending on the event detail page
- Attending events syncs to your Neighborhood Feed: **zaarhub.com/feed** shows your upcoming events sorted by date
- Change your mind? Cancel your RSVP anytime

### Event Sources
Events may be manually created by businesses or auto-synced from external providers (like Eventbrite). Both types appear together in your city's events page — you'll see the same RSVP, attendee list, and detail page for either type.

## Don't See Your City?
If your city isn't listed yet, you can request it:
1. Visit the directory page of the nearest listed city
2. Look for the "Request a City" section or form
3. Enter the city name and your email
4. We'll notify you when your city goes live

## Call-to-Action Buttons on Listings

When you view a business listing, you may see buttons that let you take action right away. These buttons are set by the business owner to match what they offer.

### Types of CTA Buttons You Might See

| Button | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Get a Quote** | Opens a form to request a price estimate from the business |
| **Book Now** | Takes you to a booking page where you can pick a date and time — the business's calendar syncs automatically |
| **Call Now** | Taps to call the business directly from your phone |
| **Visit Website** | Opens the business's website in a new tab |
| **Message Us** | Opens the messaging form to contact the business |
| **Join Rewards** | Links to the business's loyalty or rewards program (if they use IncentiveSwift) |
| **Claim Deal** | Takes you to an active deal or special offer |
| **View Menu** | Opens the business's menu (for restaurants and food services) |
| **Get Directions** | Opens a map with directions to the business location |
| **Email Us** | Opens your email app with the business's address filled in |
| **Download App** | Links to the business's mobile app in your app store |
| **Donate** | Opens a donation page (for nonprofits and charities) |
| **Register** | Opens a registration form (for classes, workshops, or events) |

### Gated CTA Buttons

Some buttons may require you to log in before you can use them. For example, a "Book Now" button might ask you to sign in first — this lets the business save your booking to your account. You'll see a prompt to log in or create a free account, then you'll be returned to complete your action.

### Why Businesses Use These

CTA buttons help you get what you need faster — no hunting for a phone number or website link. The business sets the button that best matches how they serve customers.

## Community Polls
Vote on local questions right from your directory page:
- Quick one-question polls about your community ("Best taco spot?", "Which event should we host?")
- Vote once per poll — change your vote anytime
- Live results show percentage bars after you vote
- Active polls appear in the sidebar of your city page

## Neighborhood Feed
Your personalized homepage at **zaarhub.com/feed** — everything tailored to you:
- **Greeting** — time-of-day welcome with your name
- **Saved Places** — your bookmarks at a glance, tap to revisit
- **Upcoming Events** — events you're attending, sorted by date soonest first
- **Active Polls** — vote right from the feed, see live percentages
- **Suggested Businesses** — businesses in categories you've shown interest in (from your bookmarks and survey answers)

Your feed updates as you save more businesses, RSVP to events, and vote in polls.

## Supporting Local
Every discovery, bookmark, deal claim, review, and poll vote supports your local economy.
