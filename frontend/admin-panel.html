⭐ Loyalty Programmes

One programme for the whole ZaarHub network. ZaarHub is a single directory spread across every city, so ZaarCash 💎 is earned network-wide — a shopper keeps the same balance in Palm Bay, St Cloud or any city you add later. A member earns ZaarCash per redemption when they redeem a deal, per check-in when they check in, and per visit on visit recognition. Set a rate to 0 to switch that source off.
Guardrails — every one a per-programme setting edited right here: per $1 spent is the earn rate on a member's spend, max % of a bill caps how much of a bill the currency may pay, minimum balance to redeem is what a member must hold before spending any, and free items earn nothing stops a 100%-off deal from minting currency. 100 units = US$1.00, so each rate below is also shown in dollars.
