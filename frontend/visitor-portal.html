💡 How ZaarCash Rewards Work click to expand

🎁 How You Earn

  • 1 ZaarCash per $1 you spend at any participating business
  • Show your QR code at checkout — the business scans it
  • $30 meal = 30 ZC (worth $0.30)
  • $100 shopping = 100 ZC (worth $1.00)
  • Earn at the pizzeria, the hardware store, the salon — anywhere in the directory

💸 How You Use It

  • 100 ZC = $1.00 off your bill
  • Can cover up to 10% of any purchase
  • $50 bill → use up to 500 ZC ($5 off)
  • Must have at least 100 ZC saved before redeeming
  • Use at any store — not just where you earned it
🔑 Key point: ZaarCash isn't meant to make you rich — it's a thank-you for being part of the community. After 10 visits spending $300 total, you'll have about $3.00 in ZaarCash to put toward your next purchase. Small but meaningful.
