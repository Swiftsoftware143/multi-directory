🏪 Business Owner Guide

Everything you need to manage your business listing, ads, and subscriptions through the Business Portal.

1. Introduction

The Business Portal at /portal is your command center for managing your business listing. From here you can update your profile, track performance, manage your subscription, submit ads, and respond to customer reviews.

📊 Dashboard

See visits, reviews, and subscription status at a glance.

📢 Advertising

Submit ads for admin approval and track earnings.

⭐ Reviews

⬆️ Subscriptions

Upgrade your plan for premium features and visibility.

💡 First time here? Visit /grow to see feature tiers, plan comparisons, pricing, and testimonials from other business owners. Then use /claim to start the 3-step claiming process or /biz/:slug/:id to view your live business detail page with gallery, maps, and reviews.

2. Claiming Your Business

If your business is already listed in the directory, you need to claim it before you can access the Business Portal. If your business isn't listed yet, contact the directory admin or submit a new listing through the directory's submission form.

How to Claim

Two ways to claim your business:

Option A: Inline Claim (from business detail page)

  1. Visit your business's detail page at /biz/:slug/:id.
  2. Click the Claim This Business button near the top of the page.
  3. Fill in the claim form with your contact information and submit.
  4. Wait for admin approval. You'll receive an email once approved.
  5. Set up your Business Portal password and log in at /portal.

Option B: Dedicated Claim Flow (/claim)

1

Search — Go to /claim and search for your business by name or URL. Results span all directories.

2

Verify — Select your listing and provide verification (email domain matching business website, phone number on file, etc.).

3

Confirm — Review the claim summary, agree to terms, and submit. Track status from your portal.

3. Signing In

  1. Open your browser and navigate to https://your-platform.com/portal.
  2. The login screen shows tabs for Login and Register.
  3. If you've already claimed your business, click the Login tab.
  4. Enter your email and password.
  5. Click Sign In. Your dashboard loads automatically.

💡 Tip: If you manage multiple businesses, you'll see a business selector after logging in. Select the business you want to manage from the dropdown.

First-Time Registration

If you haven't claimed your business yet but want to pre-register:

  1. Go to /portal and click the Register tab.
  2. Create your account with name, email, and password.
  3. After registration, you'll be prompted to link an existing business (search by name) or request a new listing.

6. Submitting Advertisements

As a business owner, you can submit advertisements to promote your business. Ads go through an admin approval process before being displayed.

How to Submit an Ad

1

Log into the Business Portal at /portal.

2

Select your business from the dropdown (if you have multiple businesses).

3

Scroll to the 📢 Promote Your Business section.

4

Click + New Ad to open the ad submission form.

5

Fill in the following fields:

Ad NameA short, descriptive name for your ad (e.g., "Summer Special")
Slot SizeSelect from available ad zones (Hero Banner 728×90, Sidebar 300×250, Footer 468×60)
Image URLURL to your ad image. Must match the zone's dimensions.
Target URLWhere visitors go when they click your ad (optional).
6

Click Submit Ad for Review. Your ad enters the approval queue.

⚠️ Image Requirements: Your ad image must match the selected zone's exact dimensions. A 728×90 image is required for the Hero Banner zone, 300×250 for Sidebar, and 468×60 for Footer. Images that don't match will be rejected.

Available Ad Zones

Zone NameDimensionsLocationPrice
Hero Banner728 × 90 pxTop of directory pagesPer admin pricing
Sidebar300 × 250 pxSide panel on search resultsPer admin pricing
Footer468 × 60 pxBottom of directory pagesPer admin pricing

Available zones and pricing are displayed in the slot size dropdown when you create an ad.

7. Ad Status & Earnings

Viewing Submitted Ads

After submitting ads, they appear in the My Ads list section. Each ad shows:

  • Ad Name — The name you gave the ad.
  • Zone Info — Which slot size and location.
  • Status — One of three statuses:
⏳ Pending ReviewYour ad is in the approval queue. The admin needs to review it.
✅ ApprovedYour ad is approved and live on the directory. 🎉
❌ RejectedYour ad was rejected. The rejection reason is shown next to the status.

If rejected, review the reason, fix the issue, and submit a new ad.

Viewing Ad Earnings

Once your ads are approved and running, you can track earnings:

  1. Navigate to the 📢 Promote Your Business section in the portal.
  2. Locate the 💰 Ad Earnings box (it appears automatically when you have active schedules with earnings).
  3. The earnings box shows:
    • Total Earned — Cumulative earnings from all your approved ads.
    • Active Schedules — Number of ad schedules currently running.

💡 Tip: Earnings update periodically. Check back regularly to see how your ads are performing. Create multiple ads for different zones to maximize visibility.

8. Managing Reviews

Customer reviews build trust and help your business stand out. You can view all reviews and respond to them from the portal.

Viewing Reviews

Your reviews are displayed in the Reviews section of the portal.

  • Each review shows the reviewer's name, star rating, text, and date.
  • Reviews are sorted by date, newest first.
  • The dashboard header shows your total review count and average rating.

Responding to a Review

1

Go to the Reviews section in the portal.

2

Find the review you want to respond to.

3

Click Reply below the review.

4

Type your response and click Submit Reply.

5

Your reply appears publicly below the review on the business detail page.

💡 Tip: Always respond professionally to both positive and negative reviews. Thank customers for positive feedback and address concerns in negative ones. A thoughtful response shows you care about customer experience.

Review Best Practices

  • Respond quickly — Aim to respond within 24-48 hours.
  • Be professional — Keep responses courteous and helpful.
  • Address specifics — Reference what the reviewer mentioned.
  • Take it offline — For complaints, offer to discuss further via phone or email.
  • Don't argue — If a review is unfair, respond professionally without being defensive.
  • Encourage reviews — Ask satisfied customers to leave a review to build your rating.

9. ZaarHub Loyalty Program & Scanner

As a ZaarHub business, you can participate in the ZaarHub Rewards loyalty program powered by IncentiveSwift. Customers earn ZaarCash at your business and redeem it for discounts — driving repeat visits. Your staff process transactions using the mobile Scanner PWA.

How Loyalty Appears on Your Profile

Every business profile page at /biz/:slug/:id includes a "ZaarHub Rewards" section that shows:

  • Your program details — Currency name (ZaarCash), earn rate per $1 spent
  • Customer's loyalty status — When logged in, customers see their tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum), balance, and scannable QR code
  • "Join ZaarHub Rewards" button for new customers who haven't enrolled yet
  • "🔍 Scan to Earn" button linking to the scanner for your staff

The loyalty section fetches live data from the IncentiveSwift loyalty engine via the Multi-Directory API proxy. No separate app needed — it renders inline on your business listing.

Using the ZaarHub Scanner for Transactions

The scanner at https://zaarhub.com/scanner is your staff's point-of-sale loyalty tool. It works on any device with a camera — phone, tablet, or laptop.

Quick setup:

  1. Go to zaarhub.com/scanner on your device
  2. Sign in with your business credentials
  3. The scanner auto-detects your business name and category
  4. Add it to your phone's home screen for instant one-tap access (it's a PWA)

Three scan types available:

✅ Check-inAward 10 bonus points for visiting. No purchase amount needed.
💳 PurchaseCustomer earns ZaarCash based on purchase total. Enter the amount.
🎁 RedeemCustomer uses their ZaarCash balance. Points are deducted.

Transaction flow: Select scan type → Scan customer's QR code → Enter amount (for Purchase/Redeem) → Click Process → Points awarded instantly.

💡 Tip: The scanner auto-generates a unique transaction ID for every scan — great for audit trails and dispute resolution. All data flows to the IncentiveSwift clearinghouse for accurate billing.

10. Verification Badges & Trust Signals

Once your business is claimed and verified, you earn trust badges that help visitors identify your business as legitimate and reputable. These badges display prominently on your listing card and detail page.

Available Badges

✅ Verified Owner

Automatically awarded when your claim is approved. Shows you're the legitimate owner. This is the most important badge — it tells visitors the listing is maintained by the real business.

11. Posting Deals & Events

Drive foot traffic and engagement by posting deals and events for your business. These appear on your listing, in the ZaarHub carousel, and in the community events feed.

Creating a Deal / Coupon

1

Log into the Business Portal at /portal and go to the Deals tab.

2

Click + New Deal and fill in the deal details: title, description, discount type (percentage or fixed amount), original price vs. deal price, and optional coupon code.

3

Set the start and end dates. Deals auto-activate and auto-expire — no manual management needed.

4

Toggle Featured if you want the deal highlighted in the ZaarHub carousel and directory spotlight section.

5

Click Publish Deal. It appears on your listing and in the deals feed immediately.

Creating an Event

1

Go to the Events tab in your portal dashboard.

2

Click + New Event and enter the event name, date/time, location, description, and optional image.

3

Select a category (e.g., Workshop, Sale, Community, Networking) to help visitors find it.

4

Click Publish Event. It appears in the community events feed and on your business listing.

💡 Tip: Featured deals get priority placement in the ZaarHub carousel. If you're running a limited-time promotion, mark it as Featured for maximum visibility. Events with images get 2× more RSVPs than text-only event listings.

12. Data Freshness on Your Listing

Visitors see a Data Freshness Timestamp on your business listing that shows when your information was last verified. This builds trust by showing visitors that your data is current.

  • What visitors see: A small badge or text on your listing like "Data last updated: August 2026 from Google Places" or "Verified 2 weeks ago."
  • Automatic updates: When the directory platform enriches your listing from third-party sources (Google Places, Yelp), the timestamp is updated automatically.
  • Manual refresh: After you update your profile in the Business Portal, your data freshness timestamp updates to reflect the most recent change.
  • Why it matters: Listings with recent freshness timestamps get more clicks, calls, and visits. Visitors trust active, maintained listings more than stale ones.

💡 Tip: Keep your listing data accurate. Update your hours before holidays, add seasonal photos, and verify your phone number regularly. Every update refreshes your data timestamp, signaling to visitors that you're an active business.

13. URL Cleanliness

The platform automatically cleans your website URL to keep it tidy and professional. When you submit your website during claiming or profile editing, the system strips tracking parameters before storing it.

  • What gets removed: Over 30 tracking parameters including utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, fbclid, ref, gclid, and more.
  • How it displays: On server-rendered pages, your URL shows as a clean domain name — e.g., "aspendental.com" instead of a long UTM-laden URL. It looks professional and is easier for visitors to read and remember.
  • No action needed: URL cleaning happens automatically. You don't need to do anything — just submit your normal website URL and the system handles the rest.
  • What stays: The core domain and path are preserved. Only tracking/analytics query parameters are removed.

14. Near-Me Visibility

Visitors can search for businesses near their current location using the Near-Me Geolocation feature. This helps nearby customers find your business when they're in the area.

  • How it works: Visitors click the "Near Me" button or allow location access in their browser. The directory shows businesses sorted by distance from their current position.
  • What you need to do: Make sure your business address is accurate and complete. The geolocation feature uses your listed address to calculate distance — an incomplete or outdated address means you won't appear in near-me results.
  • Impact on visibility: Businesses with accurate addresses appear in near-me results for visitors within the search radius. This is especially valuable for restaurants, retail, and service businesses where proximity matters.

⚠️ Important: If your address changes, update it immediately in your Business Portal. A wrong address means you'll show up in near-me results for the wrong location — or not show up at all for visitors near your actual location.

15. Role Switcher

The header navigation includes a Role Switcher dropdown that lets you toggle between different platform views without logging out and back in.

  • Visitor view — Browse directories, search businesses, and save favorites as a regular user.
  • Business Owner view — Access your Business Portal, manage your listing, post deals and events, and track performance.
  • Supplier view — If you're also registered as a supplier, switch to manage your product catalog, orders, and business connections.

Look for the role switcher icon in the top-right header area. Click it to see available roles based on your account. No need to maintain multiple logins — one account, multiple roles.

💡 Tip: If you manage both a business listing and a supplier account, the role switcher makes it easy to jump between managing your business profile and your supplier catalog. All from the same account.

16. Your Business on the Notifications Ticker

When you post a deal, get verified, or receive a glowing review, your business may appear in the Notifications Ticker — the scrolling activity bar on the directory and ZaarHub frontends. This is free visibility driven by your platform activity.

📡 What Triggers an Appearance

🏷️ New Deal PostedEach time you post a new deal or promotion, it can appear in the ticker. Include clear deal titles — they become the ticker text.
✅ Claim VerifiedWhen your business claim is approved, a ticker entry announces your verified status to visitors.
⭐ New ReviewHigh-rated reviews (4+ stars) may trigger a ticker mention, bringing attention to your positive customer feedback.
🔄 Profile UpdateMajor profile updates (new photos, changed hours, new services) can generate a ticker entry to let visitors know your listing is current.

📈 Maximizing Ticker Visibility

  • Post deals regularly — Each new deal is a potential ticker entry. Rotate promotions weekly to stay visible.
  • Keep your profile fresh — Update photos, hours, and services seasonally. Profile updates signal activity and may trigger ticker mentions.
  • Encourage reviews — After a great customer interaction, politely ask them to leave a review. High-rated reviews get ticker exposure.
  • Respond to reviews — Active engagement shows visitors you're attentive and may increase the likelihood of review-based ticker appearances.

💡 Tip: The ticker is fully automatic — you don't need to submit anything or pay extra. Just run your business well on the platform and the ticker picks up your activity naturally.

⚠️ Note: Ticker appearances are not guaranteed for every event. The ticker has a limited number of slots and prioritizes recent activity. An older entry may rotate out before all visitors see it.
