🛡️ Admin Guide — Managing the Loyalty Platform

For SwiftSoftware and ZaarHub administrators managing the Program backend.

🏪 1. Business Claim & Verification Flow

When a business owner claims a listing on ZaarHub, the system follows this flow:

Step 1 — Owner Submits Claim

  • Owner goes to /claim-business/ and searches for their business in the directory
  • They must find their business in search results before proceeding — no duplicate listings can be created
  • Step 2: they provide their name, email, phone, and website URL (required, validated format)

Step 2 — Auto-Approval (Instant)

The system checks two things for automatic approval:

  • Email-to-website match — The domain in the owner's email (e.g. @mybusiness.com) is checked against the submitted website domain (e.g. mybusiness.com). If they match → auto-approved
  • Direct email match — If the owner's email exactly matches the business's email field in the directory → auto-approved
  • Submitted website is saved — If the business listing had no website before, the one the claimant submits is saved to the directory automatically

When auto-approved:

  • A visitor account is created with business_type = 'merchant' and a random temp password
  • A business verification record is created with method = 'auto_domain'
  • The claimant sees their temp password on screen and can log in immediately at /portal
  • Their JWT role is set to company_admin — they see the business dashboard
✅ Auto-approval example: Owner with email john@bobsauto.com claims a business and submits website https://bobsauto.com. The domain bobsauto.com matches → instant approval.

Step 3 — Manual Review (Admin Panel)

If auto-approval fails (email domain doesn't match the website), the claim goes to the admin panel for manual review:

  1. Go to https://zaarhub.com/admin/requests.html
  2. Unlock with the admin key
  3. Click the Claims tab → review pending claims
  4. Verify the claimant's identity by checking email, phone, or website
  5. Click Approve or Reject

When manually approved via admin panel:

  • The Node.js API creates a visitor_accounts row with business_type = 'merchant'
  • A temp password is generated and sent via email (requires Mailgun configured)
  • The approval syncs to CoreSwift CRM
⚠️ No website = cannot claim: If a claimant submits without a website URL, the frontend blocks the submission. If a business listing has no website on file and no website is submitted, the claim goes to manual review with a "no website" flag.

What Business Owners See After Approval

  • Login at https://zaarhub.com/portal with their email and temp password
  • Business portal at /business-portal.html with credit rate config, purchase PIN, offers management, scanner, guides
  • They can change their password, manage their listing, create loyalty offers, and track performance

📊 2. Admin Dashboard

The admin panel is your central hub for monitoring and managing the entire ZaarHub Loyalty Program.

  • All tenants — View every merchant, supplier, and participant across the platform
  • All transactions — Monitor credit earnings, redemptions, and referral activity in real-time
  • Flagged activity — Review transactions that triggered automated fraud detection rules
  • System health — Check API status, queue lengths, and service availability
💡 Tip: Pin the dashboard to your browser. It auto-refreshes every 60 seconds so you can spot issues quickly.

👥 3. Tenant Management

As an admin, you have oversight of all tenant accounts. Your responsibilities include:

  • View accounts — Access merchant, supplier, and participant profiles when needed for support
  • Manage configurations — Adjust tenant settings when authorized (credit rates, offer limits, account status)
  • Account recovery — Help merchants and participants regain access to locked accounts
  • Account lifecycle — Activate, suspend, or deactivate accounts according to Program rules
⚠️ Important: Never make configuration changes without documented authorization. All changes should be logged with the requesting party, reason, and approval.

🔍 4. Fraud Monitoring

Fraud detection is one of the most critical admin functions. The system flags suspicious transactions automatically, but you need to investigate.

What to look for:

  • Unusual patterns — A single customer earning credits at many different businesses in a short period, or a business seeing unusual redemption spikes
  • PIN misuse — Multiple verification failures with the same PIN, or verifications occurring at odd hours with no customers present
  • Self-referral loops — Multiple accounts referring themselves using different emails or phone numbers
  • Inflated purchase amounts — A business consistently reporting purchases much higher than their average ticket, especially when the customer is a frequent visitor
  • Collusion indicators — The same customer and business transacting many times in a day with identical amounts
🔎

Review process: Open the flagged transaction → review the customer history and business history → check for matching IPs or account creation dates → make a determination: dismiss flag, warn the party, or escalate for suspension.

🚨 Escalation: If you confirm fraud, immediately suspend the involved accounts and document everything. Notify your supervisor and the security team within 24 hours.

🎧 5. Support & Escalation

Admins handle a variety of support requests from tenants. Common scenarios:

  • Account recovery — Verify identity using email, phone, or security questions, then reset access
  • PIN resets — Generate a new Purchase PIN for merchants who've lost access or suspect compromise
  • Credit corrections — Manually adjust credit balances when system errors occur. Every manual adjustment must include an audit trail: reason, authorization, timestamp
  • Dispute resolution — Mediate between participants and merchants when a transaction is disputed. Review system logs to determine the correct outcome
  • Technical support — Help tenants with portal access, scanner issues, or offer configuration
💡 Tip: For credit corrections, follow the policy: under 1,000 credits = admin-level approval; 1,000+ credits = supervisor authorization required.

📋 6. Audit & Logging

The ZaarHub Platform maintains a comprehensive audit trail. All admin actions are logged and subject to review.

  • All actions logged — Every verification, credit adjustment, account modification, and offer change is recorded with the admin who performed it
  • Manual adjustments documented — When you make a manual credit correction or account change, the system records the before/after state, reason, and your admin ID
  • Regular audit reviews — Audit logs are reviewed on a recurring schedule to ensure compliance with policies
  • Retention — Logs are retained for a minimum of 3 years per regulatory requirements
⚠️ Important: Assume everything you do as an admin is auditable and visible. Never perform actions outside official channels or for personal benefit.

🔒 7. Security Best Practices

Admin accounts have elevated privileges. Follow these practices to keep the platform secure:

  • MFA is required — Multi-factor authentication must be enabled on your admin account. No exceptions
  • No personal account access — Never use admin tools to access your own personal account or accounts of friends/family. If you need customer support, submit a ticket like anyone else
  • Report breaches immediately — If you suspect any security incident (compromised credentials, data exposure, unauthorized access), report it to the security team immediately. Do not investigate on your own
  • Least privilege — Only access the minimum data needed for your current task. Don't browse accounts or transactions out of curiosity
  • Secure your session — Always log out when leaving your workstation. Don't share your admin session with anyone
🚨 Non-compliance: Violations of security policies are grounds for immediate revocation of admin access, termination of employment or contract, and possible legal action.

  • Confirm or Decline buttons on pending requests

👥 11. Referrals & Zaarcash

Admins verify and manage referrals:

  • Referrals tab — tabbed view: Pending | Verified | Paid
  • Pending — shows referrer, referee, direction, and action buttons
  • Verify button — marks referral as paid and grants Zaarcash to referrer via the MultiDirectory loyalty engine
  • Reject button — marks as rejected
  • Summary cards: Total referrals, Pending count, Total Zaarcash awarded
  • Amounts by direction: visitor→visitor: 50, business→business: 200 (2×), business→visitor: 50, visitor→business: 100

🔌 12. Integration CTA Buttons

Business owners configure CTA buttons on their listing from the Integrations tab:

  • Booking CTA — toggles CoreSwift booking widget, selects CTA from pre-approved list
  • Campaign CTA — toggles the MultiDirectory loyalty engine campaign widget, selects CTA
  • SMS CTA — toggles FunnelSwift SMS funnel, selects CTA
  • All CTAs use controlled dropdowns — no free text, keeps directory branding consistent
  • Buttons only render on the business detail page when toggled on

🏅 13. Verification Badges & Trust Signals

Businesses in the directory can earn verification badges that display on their listings. These badges help visitors identify trusted businesses at a glance:

  • Verified Owner — automatically awarded when a business owner completes the claim verification process and is confirmed as the legitimate owner
  • Top Rated — awarded to businesses that maintain a high average rating (4.5+ stars) across a minimum number of reviews
  • Editor's Pick — manually awarded by admins to businesses they want to feature (see Section 14 for Editor's Picks management)

Badge display: Badges appear as icons next to the business name on listing cards, search results, and the business detail page. They are controlled by the ZaarHub system and cannot be manually toggled by business owners (except Editor's Pick, which is admin-controlled).

💡 Tip: Use verification badges strategically. The "Verified Owner" badge is automatic once a claim is approved. Top Rated badges update automatically as new reviews come in. Editor's Pick is your manual curation tool for highlighting standout businesses.

⭐ 14. Editor's Picks Configuration

Editor's Picks are featured businesses hand-selected by admins to appear in a special "Editor's Picks" section on the directory and ZaarHub frontend. They're part of the ZaarHub curation system.

  • Add an Editor's Pick — Go to the business listing admin panel, find the business, and click Mark as Editor's Pick. The business receives the Editor's Pick badge.
  • Display order — Editor's Picks can be reordered via drag-and-drop in the admin panel. The order controls the display sequence on the frontend.
  • Remove — Click the Editor's Pick badge or status toggle to remove curation. The badge disappears immediately from the public listing.
  • ZaarHub visibility — Editor's Picks appear in the ZaarHub carousel alongside featured deals and spotlights, giving them cross-directory exposure.
⚠️ Note: Editor's Picks are purely a curation tool — they don't change the business's subscription tier or billing. Businesses don't pay for Editor's Pick status; it's earned through admin recognition of quality.

📋 15. B2B Pages Monitoring

Three server-rendered B2B pages are available for your directory network. As an admin, you should monitor their usage and content:

  • RFQ Marketplace (/rfq-marketplace) — Businesses post Request-for-Quote listings. Review RFQ volume and supplier response rates periodically to gauge marketplace health.
  • Co-op Hub (/coop-hub) — Cooperative purchasing and partnership hub. Monitor which directories are participating and flag any inappropriate partnership listings.
  • Lead Exchange (/lead-exchange) — Business lead marketplace. Watch for lead quality and ensure all participants follow directory terms of service.
💡 Tip: All three B2B pages are server-side rendered for SEO and fast loading. They use the same design system as the rest of the platform. Check each page after major deployments to ensure rendering is working correctly.

🕐 16. Data Freshness & Enrichment

Business listings enriched from third-party sources (Google Places, Yelp, etc.) display a Data Freshness Timestamp to visitors. For example: "Data last updated: June 2025 from Google Places."

  • Automatic timestamps — When a listing is enriched via the Data Company enrichment engine, the enrichment date and source are recorded and displayed on the public listing.
  • Stale data alerts — Listings that haven't been refreshed in 6+ months are flagged in the admin panel. You can trigger a re-enrichment from the dashboard.
  • Manual refresh — Use the Enrich button on individual business listings to pull fresh data from providers and update the timestamp.
  • Visitor confidence — Freshness badges increase visitor trust. Listings with "Updated this month" tags see higher engagement than those with outdated data.
💡 Tip: Set up a recurring n8n workflow to auto-enrich listings older than 90 days. This keeps your directory data fresh without manual effort.

📡 17. Notifications Ticker

The Notifications Ticker is a scrolling bar that automatically displays recent platform activity on the directory frontend. Unlike admin-created announcements, this ticker populates automatically from real platform events — new businesses added, deals posted, reviews written, and claims verified.

  • Enable / Disable — Go to Settings → Notifications Ticker and toggle the ticker on or off. When disabled, the ticker bar is completely hidden from all frontend pages.
  • Position — Choose top (below the header navigation bar) or bottom (above the site footer). Default is top.
  • Scroll speed — Set to Slow (60s cycle), Normal (30s), or Fast (15s). Slower speeds are more readable; faster speeds show more items.
  • Max items — Limit how many entries appear in the ticker queue. Default is 5, maximum is 20. Older items rotate out as new events occur.
  • Event filters — Toggle which event types appear: new businesses, new deals, new reviews, and verified claims. Turn off types you don't want displayed.
  • Customization — Ticker colors, font size, and animation speed can be overridden via the Theme → CSS Variables panel. Variables: --ticker-bg, --ticker-text, --ticker-accent, --ticker-font-size, --ticker-speed.
💡 Tip: The Notifications Ticker and the Announcement Ticker (Section 30 of the main guide) serve different purposes. The Notifications Ticker is fully automatic — it shows system events with no admin input. Use the Announcement Ticker when you want to publish custom messages like holiday hours or event promotions.

📂 18. Category Management

Categories are organized into groups (e.g., Dining, Home & Outdoor, Professional) and each group contains individual categories. A business can belong to multiple categories (up to 3 by default, 5 with admin override).

⚠️ Critical: Categories must be created before searching for or adding businesses. The search engine filter dropdown is populated from existing categories. If no categories exist for a directory, the category filter will be disabled.

Step 1: Create Categories First

Before populating a directory with businesses, create your categories from the Category Manager (Admin Panel → Categories). Each category has a name, slug, and group. Categories are available immediately after creation.

Step 2: Search for Businesses

Go to Admin Panel → Search & Import. The search engine includes a Category Filter dropdown that shows all existing categories. Select a category (or group) to narrow your search. The filter makes results highly targeted from the start — example: select "Coffee Shop" and the search returns coffee shops in the target city.

Step 3: Assign Categories — Individual or Bulk

After search results load, you have two ways to assign categories:

Individual assignment:

  • Each search result row has a category dropdown. The admin selects one or more categories from the dropdown.
  • Click Save on that row to update the business's categories immediately.

Bulk assignment:

  • Select multiple businesses using the checkboxes in the search results table.
  • Click the Bulk Actions button at the top of the table.
  • Choose "Assign Categories" from the bulk actions menu.
  • Select one or more target categories in the modal.
  • Choose Replace (overwrites existing) or Append (adds to existing).
  • Click Apply — all selected businesses update in one operation.
💡 Tip: Use bulk assign as an auto-categorization tool. Search by one category filter, assign those results, then search by another filter and assign again. Businesses that appear in both searches get both categories — giving them natural multi-category coverage.

Other Assignment Methods

  • CSV import — Import templates include a category column. Map columns to existing categories during the import wizard.
  • Post-creation editing — Edit any business from the admin panel and modify its categories via the multi-select. Changes take effect immediately.

Business Owner Self-Service Categories

After claiming their business, owners can manage categories through the Business Portal:

  • Removing a category — Instant. Owner clicks "Remove" next to any assigned category. No admin approval needed.
  • Adding a category — Requires admin approval. Owner browses categories and clicks "Request." Creates a pending request visible under Pending Category Requests.
  • Maximum categories — Default cap is 3 per business. Admins can override to 5 for verified/top-rated businesses.
  • Primary vs. secondary — The first category is the primary and receives full search weight. Additional categories at 75% weight to prevent gaming.

Approving Category Requests

Go to Pending Category Requests in the admin sidebar. Each request shows business name, current categories, requested category, and optional rationale. One-click Approve or Deny with optional denial reason.

Category Cleanup & Merging

  • Rename — Rename any category; all business associations preserved.
  • Delete — Only empty categories (0 businesses) can be deleted.
  • Merge — Select two categories and click "Merge Into." All businesses from source are re-assigned to target, source deleted.
  • Group reassignment — Move a category between groups by editing its group name.
